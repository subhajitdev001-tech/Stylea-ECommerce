About STYLÉA

STYLÉA is an online fashion store from Haldia • Kolkata. You can shop for both men and women, with collections like sarees, dresses, shirts, T-shirts, jeans, shoes, jewellery and watches, all in one place.

Not sure what to wear? Try our AI Stylist on the home page to get outfit ideas for different occasions, then add your favourites to your wishlist or cart.

How Shopping Works

1

Create an account

Register or log in. You need an account to use the wishlist, cart and checkout.

2

Browse and choose

Explore categories for men and women. Tap the heart to save an item to your wishlist.

3

Add several items to your cart and check out together, or use Buy Now to purchase a single item quickly.

4

Enter your delivery address

Fill in your name, phone number, full address and pincode. Please double-check them so your order reaches you.

5

Choose how to pay

Pay online with UPI, or choose Cash on Delivery. See Payment below for details.

Track your order

After ordering, open Orders from your avatar menu to see its status.

Payment Information

Online Payment (UPI)

FASTEST
  • Pay with UPI apps like Google Pay, PhonePe and Paytm.
  • When you place your order, a secure payment window (powered by Razorpay) opens. Complete your payment there.
  • Your order is confirmed once the payment succeeds, and you'll see the success page.
  • Your payment details are entered on Razorpay's window and are not stored on our website.

Cash on Delivery

  • Choose Cash on Delivery at checkout and pay in cash when your order arrives.
  • Your order is placed right away, and payment shows as "Pending" until you pay on delivery.
  • Please keep the exact amount ready if you can.

Prices & Totals

At checkout you'll see the original price, your discount, and how much you save. The total shown there is the amount you pay, with free delivery.

Money deducted but no order showing? Please don't pay again. Online orders appear in Orders only after the payment is confirmed. Contact us with the payment date, amount and payment ID (or a screenshot) and we'll check it for you.

Returns & Refunds

We offer 7-day returns. If something isn't right, contact us within 7 days of receiving your order with your order number, the item and the reason, and we'll guide you through the next steps.

Once a return is approved, refunds for online payments are sent back to the original payment method. For Cash on Delivery orders, we'll arrange the refund with you directly.

Quick Answers

How do I change my password or avatar?

Hover over your avatar in the top-right corner and click Manage Account. There you can pick a new avatar and change your password. You'll stay logged in.

I forgot my password

Please contact us from the email address you registered with, and we'll help you get back into your account.

Why can't I add items to my cart or wishlist?

You need to be logged in to use the cart, wishlist and checkout. Log in or create an account and try again.

Where can I see my past orders?

Click Orders in your avatar menu. Every order shows its items, date, payment method, total and current status.

Still need help?

Send us a message and we'll get back to you by email.
